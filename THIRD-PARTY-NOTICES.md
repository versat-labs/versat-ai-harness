# Third-party notices

Retain the [reviewed source-distribution notices](docs/licensing/candidates/apache-2.0/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md) and [full upstream license texts](docs/licensing/technical-review/dependency-license-texts/). They identify separately installed dependencies, actual observed versions and conditional distribution obligations. Apache does not replace third-party terms. This index adds no license clause or grant.

The review history records candidate approval separately from effective adoption. This private rehearsal tree is not an adopted public release. Before any authorized distribution, verify the actual bundled contents and notices against the final approved tree.
