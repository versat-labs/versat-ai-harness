## Prepare and review

Identify the exact component/revision, original and third-party material, intended distribution and applicable grants/notices. The [canonical policy](../../../standards/core/licensing-publication.md) governs this review. Authorized reviewers inspect sensitive evidence in restricted storage; this public-facing route does not require private records or training to understand the process.

The proposed Apache terms permit general and commercial use, modification, redistribution and forks of covered material under their conditions. Third-party material keeps its own terms. The tool does not impose its license on unrelated destination-project material; copied templates still carry applicable obligations. No separate CLA, DCO or IP assignment is introduced. The official English text governs the candidate, including contribution, patent, notice, trademark and warranty conditions; translations here are explanations.

For this Harness only, the approved R2 handoff selects GitHub Free with a bounded sequence: complete private clearance and exact adoption/integration first, then separately authorize public visibility and immediate main protection/readback within 15 minutes. Actual public enforcement, reporting and external-use checks remain mandatory before later release. This scoped timing exception does not waive rights, confidentiality or human review and does not change the canonical Standard. See the [current handoff](../../handoffs/harness-publication-blockers/README.md).

## Try one review

Example: a copied template has no verified grant. Record its revision, missing evidence, responsible owner and condition for reconsideration; set disposition to `defer`. A public URL, owner label, clean scan or passing validator is not proof of permission. Expected result: an attributable review decision, not an applied license.

## Recover and hand off

Missing rights, actual text approval or required private checks keeps preparation incomplete. Confidential history or unknown controls defers publication even when a negative assessment is documented. Review the full exposed repository/service surface, not only the current files. Preserve historical evidence and request a separate remediation decision when necessary.

Text approval, exact patch review, adoption, release/visibility, external-user checks and consumer updates are separate events. Other products retain their own terms and evidence. See [preparation status](../../handoffs/harness-publication-readiness/README.md) and [contribution guidance](contributing.md).
