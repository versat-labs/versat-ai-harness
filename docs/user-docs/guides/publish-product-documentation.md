# Publish user documentation and feature pages

[English](publish-product-documentation.md) | [Português](publish-product-documentation.pt.md) | [Español](publish-product-documentation.es.md)

Use this guide when a feature is ready for its intended readers. The feature page helps them decide whether it solves their problem. The user guide helps them complete the task and recognize the result. Keep both tied to the same supported behavior and approved source revision.

## Prepare and publish

1. Assign a product owner for the content and a publishing operator. Record the reader, the problem, when the feature helps, and the intended access. Identify a marketing page and a user guide for each applicable feature, then link them from the product overview or first-use guide as appropriate.
2. Choose destinations the intended readers can reach. The product's docs site, help center or private portal can hold guides. The approved marketing site or product catalog can hold feature pages. Record the source paths and the revision to publish. Keep credentials and restricted discovery evidence in approved storage.
3. Write the canonical English material and align the Portuguese and Spanish versions. The feature page explains the benefit and links to the appropriate guide. The guide explains preparation, an example action, the expected result, and relevant limits or recovery. Open every language link and compare claims, steps and outcomes. For an English-only technical reference, identify its language. See [project validation (English)](../../guides/project-validation.md#documentation-reporting) for the mechanical PR report.
4. Review ownership, audience, disclosure and applicable terms under the [Licensing and Publication Standard (English)](../../../standards/core/licensing-publication.md). Obtain the applicable content and access approval before release. The authorized operator previews and publishes the approved revision through the chosen destination's normal process.
5. Verify from the intended reader's access level. Open the overview, feature page and guide in each applicable language, follow their links, execute the representative action and compare the observed result. Record the published revision, URLs and verification outcome. If the result or access is wrong, restore the previous approved revision or withdraw the affected pages, record the issue and verify the correction.

Keep a feature-to-page-and-guide mapping with the owner and revision. When behavior changes, update the source and language versions together and repeat the affected verification. If approval, access or a destination is missing, record publication as pending with the reason, owner and next action. Record a reason when publication does not apply.

## Worked examples

**Publishable feature — scheduled export.** The Reporting team owns the content. An approved revision links the feature page in the product catalog to the help center guide in each language. The page explains why scheduled delivery helps a reporting team. The guide has the reader schedule one export and confirm that the schedule shows the next run. The operator publishes the approved versions and verifies the links and result as a reader. If a localized guide gives an incorrect result, restore its previous approved revision and coordinate the correction across all affected pages.

**Restricted feature — support replay.** The Support team needs a guide for authorized staff, but a restricted destination has yet to be approved. Record publication as pending, with the Support team as owner and destination/access approval as the next action. Keep the draft in approved restricted storage. After approval, verify that authorized staff can open it and that readers without access cannot.
