## Product and ownership

Versat AI Harness helps developers, reviewers and maintainers turn development intent into reviewed, traceable changes. Versat maintains it; @elviszoz and @evertonspohrversat are technical review contacts in CODEOWNERS. The intended public audience includes external developers. The existing repository remains private while public distribution is prepared.

## Problem, job and persona

A contributor needs to identify the correct project, prepare a first SDD plan, understand the human gates and supply current validation evidence. Maintained instructions should make those steps understandable without private recordings or organization membership. Actual repository/tool access remains a separate prerequisite; this document does not grant it.

The original Versat team onboarding need is recorded in [Issue #22](https://github.com/versat-labs/versat-ai-harness/issues/22), delivered history whose access follows the repository visibility. It is provenance, not a prerequisite for using this guide. The frequency and cost of that need remain unmeasured; validate them with team feedback after first use. This motivates the current documentation work without claiming new market evidence.

## Solution and value

One versioned root Plugin combines canonical Standards, the SDD lifecycle, five Skills, composable templates and deterministic validation. It supports bootstrap, changes, maintenance, audit and architecture review. Clearer decisions and reproducible checks are intended benefits; productivity and defect reductions remain outcomes to measure.

## Origin and current boundaries

The [ADRs](../decisions/README.md) record the package and process decisions. The current source includes unreleased SDD v3 assurance and explicit test execution beyond the published 0.3.0 tag. VERSION/manifests now declare the local 0.4.0 delivery candidate; its catalog references v0.4.0 without creating or activating that release. Marketplace identity, installed copies and consumer pins are unchanged.

The [Apache candidate](../licensing/candidates/apache-2.0/APPROVAL.md) has actual text approval and its license/notices are applied locally; main integration and public distribution remain pending. Third-party terms and unrelated destination-project licenses remain separate. Confidentiality, actual controls and external-user verification still limit public readiness. The Harness does not supply automatic publication, consumer upgrades or an operational workflow engine. Structural validation cannot authenticate human approval or prove behavior, remote CI or merge.

## Applicability and alternatives

The intended audience expands beyond Versat's team without changing other products' models. No external pricing, commission model, market sizing or financial return is established. Alternatives include scattered team instructions, project-specific checklists and generic coding assistance. The Harness combines context-resolved policy and explicit evidence/gates; that is an integration choice, not proven market superiority.

## Scalability and responsibility

Exact releases and composed templates support adoption across projects. Human review, translation upkeep, client compatibility and access remain constraints. Assess onboarding completion, time to a first reviewed plan and support questions before claiming value. Protect confidential records; private rehearsal does not prove anonymous access. Fresh test directories share permissions and are not security isolation.

Accessible multilingual instructions may reduce participation barriers. Model use and repeated evaluations consume resources; measure their impact before making environmental-efficiency claims.
