### Resolution workflow and exact exit

Use an evidence-led review workflow, not a new runtime service or general scanner. Reuse canonical Standards, existing validators, guides, candidate patch and client fixtures. The accountable owner coordinates actual technical, rights and security reviewers in their recorded capacities. R3 changes only the reviewed general CODEOWNERS fallback to both named maintainers and preserves every specific assignment; shared triage is explicit below. The workflow is: freeze a current review boundary; open attributable blocker records; obtain missing evidence within authorized scope; review and verify permitted treatment; re-freeze changed inputs; run acceptance checks; reconcile the pre-publication decision. `defer` remains the outcome if any required pre-publication blocker is open. Completing a review memo cannot satisfy a failed resolution requirement.
